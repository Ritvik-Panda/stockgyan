StockGyan Health Score Methodology
Business Quality – 30%: ROE compared with the company's sector benchmark.
ROCE – 20%: ROCE compared with the sector benchmark.
P/E / Valuation – 20%: P/E compared with the sector benchmark. Lower relative P/E scores better.
Earnings Growth – 15%: Latest available year-on-year net-profit growth.
Financial Strength – 10%: Balance-sheet liability-to-asset strength.
Profitability – 5%: ROA relative to the sector benchmark.

The Health Score is calculated by the StockGyan backend refresh engine and stored in StockGyan D1. This screener only reads stored data; it does not make live Upstox requests. Missing fundamental data is not treated as zero.

Strategy scans only work when their required values are present in the existing API response. Optional fields are displayed as unavailable when absent; the page does not infer or fabricate them. Quality, Value and Growth quick scans are simple convenience filters and are not investment recommendations.
